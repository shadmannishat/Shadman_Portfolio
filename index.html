About Me

A multidisciplinary professional with experience across engineering, research, design, education, operations, digital systems, and leadership. My work combines analytical thinking with creativity, allowing me to work across technical, strategic, and communication-driven environments while building practical, user-focused, and innovation-oriented solutions... Today I lead IELTS and language teaching at IELTS 7+ in Dhaka, where I teach, design courses and author preparation materials for IELTS, Foundation English and Spoken English learners.

With a background in Industrial and Production Engineering, I have built experience across engineering research, CAD-based design, workflow optimization, digital education, banking operations, creative media, and organizational leadership. My professional journey reflects a combination of technical expertise, adaptability, communication skills, and problem-solving across both corporate and innovation-driven environments.

My technical experience includes CAD modeling, manufacturing concepts, process improvement, workflow analysis, industrial research, and digital system development. I have worked with SolidWorks, AutoCAD, CATIA, Fusion 360, Python, MATLAB, Excel, Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, Audition, and Premiere Pro, enabling me to work at the intersection of engineering, design, technology, and communication.

I have worked on research and development projects related to Bangladesh’s light engineering sector in collaboration with SME Foundation-Bangladesh, Swisscontact-Bangladesh, and Ministry of Industries-supported initiatives. My responsibilities included industrial data collection, workflow analysis, productivity improvement research, occupational health and safety communication, and technical documentation. I also served as the Lead CAD Designer for the “Food Solar Dryer” project, which resulted in a co-authored research publication in AgriEngineering by MDPI.

Professionally, I have experience in both operational and educational environments. At IFIC Bank PLC, I worked on workflow management, KPI monitoring, operational efficiency improvement, and service process optimization. Alongside that, my work in digital education involved curriculum development, assessment systems, content strategy, process automation, and student-focused learning solutions.

Beyond professional work, I have consistently taken leadership roles in technical, creative, and community-focused organizations. I founded CADers of IPE to promote CAD and design learning among engineering students and served in leadership positions across organizations related to research, media, youth engagement, public speaking, and student development.

My achievements include recognition in National Science Fair competitions, National Science Olympiad, public speaking events, leadership programs, and innovation-focused activities. Across every field I work in, I aim to combine engineering logic, creativity, communication, and strategic thinking to create meaningful impact through technology, research, design, and innovation-driven problem solving.
